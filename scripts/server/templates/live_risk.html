Live Risk & Rebalance

Your actual IB holdings versus strategy targets: risk metrics, drift, and the paper trail for your monthly manual rebalance.

How the monthly workflow fits together
  1. Check Current Holdings for risk and concentration.
  2. Set/adjust your Preferred Blend (weighted mix of strategies you trust).
  3. Size it with a budget to get a target book in £ and shares.
  4. Check Drift vs Target to see what's out of line.
  5. Enter any orders MANUALLY in IB Gateway.
  6. Record what you traded under Mark as Traded so per-strategy slices stay accurate.

Trackers = paper watchlist. Pre-registrations = frozen risk envelopes graded nightly.

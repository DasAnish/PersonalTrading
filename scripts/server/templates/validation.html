Validation & Overfitting

Statistical checks that separate genuine edge from luck, across the whole strategy library.

How to read this page

SPA / Reality Check = the library-wide go/no-go. A small p-value (p < 0.05) means the best strategy survives the "you tried hundreds of things" correction.

Per-strategy battery (DSR, MinBTL, CPCV, bootstrap) judges each strategy in isolation. Worst verdict wins.
